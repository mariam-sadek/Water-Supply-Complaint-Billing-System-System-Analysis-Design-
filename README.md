# Water Supply Complaint & Billing System

## Project Description

The Water Supply Complaint & Billing System is a system designed to help water companies manage customer complaints and billing efficiently. It allows customers to register complaints online or by phone, follow their status with a tracking number, view meter readings, receive e-bills, and pay online, while enabling technical teams and managers to track, manage, and resolve complaints.

## Project Objectives

- Organize water complaints (no water, low pressure, leaks, wrong bills, meter faults).
- Track the status of every complaint until it is solved.
- Calculate bills from actual meter consumption to reduce disputes.
- Report leaks faster to reduce water loss.
- Provide e-bills, SMS reminders, and online payment for customers.
- Give managers reports on complaints, leaks, response times, and collection.
